## My Favorite Movie: The How of Us

**The How of Us ** is a 2018 Philippine romantic drama film directed by *Cathy Garcia-Molina* and starring *Kathryn Bernardo* and *Daniel Padilla*.

### Why I Love It:
* **Idolizing:** I'm a big fan of Daniel Padilla and Kathryn Bernardo also known as loveteam *Kathniel*.
* **Plot:** The story follows a young couple, **George** and **Primo**, as they navigate the highs and lows of a long-term relationship while trying to build their dreams and maintain their shared home.
* **Characters:** * Kathryn Bernardo as *Georgina "George" Silva*
                  * Daniel Padilla as *Primo Alvarez*.
